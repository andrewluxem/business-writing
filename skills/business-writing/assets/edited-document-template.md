# Edited business document template

Use this structure for a new document or a revised draft. Remove sections that do not serve the reader, but never remove a known constraint, decision, or evidence gap.

## Document setup

- Reader: [Primary reader or decision group]
- Reader's concern: [What they need to understand, decide, or do]
- Requested outcome: [Decision, approval, action, or shared understanding]
- Source facts provided: [Facts, dates, figures, and quotes that may be used]

## Document

### [Specific title]

#### Bottom line

[State the recommendation, request, decision, or conclusion in 1-3 sentences.]

#### Why it matters

[Connect the point to a supported customer, operating, financial, or team consequence. If the consequence is unknown, write Evidence needed.]

#### Evidence and reasoning

[Present the few facts and arguments the reader needs. Put supporting detail after the point it supports.]

#### Tradeoffs and open questions

- [Tradeoff, competing view, or unresolved question]
- [Evidence needed before a final decision]

#### Next steps

| Action item | Owner | Due date | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner needed] | [Date needed] | [Observable result] |

#### Assumptions and evidence gaps

- [Labeled assumption]
- [Missing metric, date, incident, attribution, or quote]
