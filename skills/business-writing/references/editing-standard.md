# Business writing editing standard

Use this reference for a full rewrite, an audit, or a disputed edit. Work from thought to structure to sentences. Line editing cannot rescue a document whose point is missing.

## Three pillars

### Clarity of thought

- Write for a named reader. State what that reader should know, decide, or do.
- Supply enough background for that reader, not every fact the writer knows.
- Use supported specifics. Name who, how much, and when when the input provides them.
- Explain why the point matters instead of making the reader infer the consequence.
- Keep warranted uncertainty. Replace vague qualifiers only when the evidence supports a firmer statement.

### Document structure

- Put the recommendation, request, decision, or conclusion first.
- Arrange evidence under the point it supports.
- Keep one job per section and remove side arguments that do not affect the requested outcome.
- Address the obvious tradeoff or competing view.
- Move useful detail that interrupts the main argument into a clearly labeled supporting section.

### Sentence mechanics

- Prefer a visible actor and a direct verb.
- Cut repeated setup, filler, and qualitative intensifiers.
- Expand unfamiliar acronyms on first use.
- Keep terms and proper nouns consistent.
- Connect every pronoun to a clear noun.
- Correct grammar and punctuation after the argument and structure are sound.

## Five editing questions

1. Is this written for the reader?
2. Which words weaken the claim without expressing real uncertainty?
3. Which claim needs a supported figure, date, person, or example?
4. What can be cut without changing meaning?
5. Why does this point matter to the reader?

## Revision passes

1. **Truth pass.** Separate supplied facts from assumptions and missing evidence.
2. **Purpose pass.** Name the reader and requested outcome.
3. **Bottom-line pass.** Move the point or request into the opening.
4. **Reasoning pass.** Connect each supporting fact to a claim and state the consequence.
5. **Structure pass.** Reorder, combine, or remove sections.
6. **Sentence pass.** Repair voice, specificity, brevity, terms, pronouns, and mechanics.
7. **Cold-read pass.** Read aloud or from the end toward the beginning to catch skipped words and hidden assumptions.

## Score meaning

- 0 means the dimension is missing, contradicted, or harmful to the reader.
- 1 means the dimension is present but incomplete or inconsistent.
- 2 means the dimension is clear enough to support the requested outcome.

Do not total the scores into a claim of writing quality. The dimensions locate the next repair; they do not certify the document.
